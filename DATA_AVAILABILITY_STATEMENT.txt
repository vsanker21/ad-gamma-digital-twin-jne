Data availability statement. All materials required to reproduce the analyses in this article are openly available. Analysis-ready cohort tables from Etter et al. (2019; n = 10) and Iaccarino et al. (2016; n = 18), the pooled exploratory table (n = 28), pre-registration and validation summary files, Phase 2 parameter-sweep outputs, analysis scripts, and Brian2 / Digital Twin configuration files are deposited at https://github.com/vsanker21/ad-gamma-digital-twin-jne and permanently archived on Zenodo (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22746385; versioned release matching this manuscript). A machine-readable CITATION.cff file is included in the repository. Analyses used Python 3.10 with fixed random seed 42.
